## Add a gesture

For each new gesture, record:
- A unique label and plain-language meaning
- A photo or drawing of the hand pose (with permission)
- Which fingers move and how the magnets are positioned
- Typical raw sensor ranges for each sensor
- A neutral/rest pattern
- Number of trials, correct detections and observed confusion with other gestures

Do not claim a gesture is recognized reliably until it has been tested over repeated sessions. With three Hall sensors, the vocabulary must remain limited to configurations the hardware can distinguish.
